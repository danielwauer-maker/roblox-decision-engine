# R12 Portfolio Closure

Date: 2026-10-12
Status: IN PROGRESS

## Case-study thesis

Roblox Decision Engine demonstrates how a product-analytics workflow can move beyond dashboards and turn platform metrics into a bounded decision process:

**Roblox Analytics → normalized history → deterministic decision rules → evidence bundle → grounded AI explanation → experiment plan.**

The product is intentionally designed so the language model does not calculate authoritative KPIs and cannot silently turn a hypothesis into a proven cause.

## Problem

Small and mid-sized Roblox studios can access analytics but may still struggle with the product-management question that follows: **What deserves attention first, why, and what should we test next?**

The project addresses that gap with a decision layer rather than another generic dashboard.

## Product solution

- connect an authorized Roblox experience using read-only analytics access
- normalize selected retention, engagement and monetization metrics
- compare current values with baselines and segments
- rank a bounded Top-3 decision queue
- attach explicit evidence and data-quality context
- let an AI analyst explain only the supplied evidence
- convert supported findings into experiments with primary metrics and guardrails
- preserve a deterministic fallback when the model is unavailable or rejected

## What this project demonstrates

### Product analytics

The project converts retention, engagement and monetization signals into a prioritization workflow rather than stopping at visualization.

### Decision intelligence

Recommendations are traceable to structured evidence, impact/confidence scoring and explicit root-cause hypotheses.

### Applied AI engineering

The LLM is intentionally constrained to explanation. Deterministic calculations, evidence validation, post-generation guardrails, repair logic and fallback behavior keep model output subordinate to the product's evidence layer.

### Backend engineering

FastAPI, PostgreSQL, external API integration, dependency readiness, retry policy, idempotency, tests and observability form a production-oriented service baseline.

### SaaS / product thinking

The project includes pilot design, validation pricing, commercial hypotheses, privacy/security baselines and a production launch path rather than being only a technical prototype.

## Explicitly unvalidated

Portfolio closure does **not** mean market validation is complete. The following claims are intentionally not made:

- no claim of paying customers
- no claim of validated recurring revenue
- no claim that the current pricing has proven willingness to pay
- no claim of statistically proven retention or revenue lift for a real studio
- no claim of full commercial general availability

The commercial readiness score therefore remains below one hundred percent even when R12 delivery reaches completion.

## Final portfolio standard

The project can be presented as a production-oriented early-access SaaS case study with real provider validation and a public validation experience. The strongest story is not “AI generated recommendations”; it is **the system design that makes those recommendations inspectable, bounded and recoverable.**
